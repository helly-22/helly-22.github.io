About Me

HD
Helly DhameshaAI engineer · MS CS, UMass Amherst

I love the part of AI that happens after the demo. At Anumana I worked on ECG models that became part of an FDA-cleared algorithm used in US hospitals, and learned that careful evaluation is what makes a model trustworthy.

Now I build LLM agents and retrieval systems, and I research how language models behave when they make choices about people: whether they share resources fairly, and whether an assistant can recommend a product without being pushy. Before UMass, I studied CS with a specialization in AI & Robotics at VIT.
