Here is a little background

Hey! I'm Helly, an AI engineer finishing my MS in Computer Science at UMass Amherst. Before that I studied CS with a specialization in AI & Robotics at VIT in Chennai.

I love the part of AI that happens after the demo. At Anumana I worked on ECG models that became part of an FDA-cleared algorithm used in US hospitals, and learned that careful evaluation is what makes a model trustworthy. These days I build LLM agents and retrieval systems, and I research how language models behave when they make choices about people: whether they share resources fairly, and whether an assistant can recommend a product without being pushy.

Off the clock you'll find me sketching, out on a trail, writing, buried in a book, playing sports, or baking something for whoever is nearby. I also chair CSWomen at UMass and once sat on the child jury of an international film festival.
